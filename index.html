How to use these apps

  1. Open this page in your phone's browser, such as Chrome or Firefox.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots), then Add to Home screen. Some browsers word it slightly differently, such as Install.
  3. Tap Add. The SK Free Toolkit icon now appears on your home screen.
  4. Tap the SK Free Toolkit icon any time, then choose an app such as the To-Do List.
  5. To come back to this page from an app, tap SK Free Toolkit at the top left.

On iPhone, open the page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Your tasks are saved on your own phone. Nothing is sent to anyone else.
